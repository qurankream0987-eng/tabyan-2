## Gate before sending any test audio

Obtain a written agreement that specifies:

1. The contracting/service operator and exact QRC product, endpoint, authentication method, protocol version, and server-to-server permission.
2. Pricing, included usage, overages, minimum commitment, rate limits, concurrency, session duration, timeout, and support/SLA terms.
3. Audio/transcript/feedback retention, deletion timing and scope, model-training/evaluation use, subprocessors, processing region, security commitments, DPA, and incident handling.
4. Rights and permissions for one controlled private acceptance sample and any later comparison. Do not publish a benchmark unless the agreement explicitly permits it.
5. A Tabyan-owned learner notice/consent and session-start policy for GENERAL and EDUCATIONAL sessions.

No vendor contact was made in this readiness work. These questions remain open; this document does not infer a privacy guarantee from a marketing or working-policy summary.
