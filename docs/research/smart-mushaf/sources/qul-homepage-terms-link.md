OVERVIEW

## Reigniting    the Islamic Golden Age.

QUL— Quranic Universal Library, provides a comprehensive, centralized hub of essential digital Quran resources, allowing you to go from idea to impact much faster.


[GET STARTED](https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources)

Islamic projects are built upon the same universal resources: the Quran, its translations, its tafsir, recitations of it, and related content and data.


To save you time and effort when it comes to sourcing, formatting, and auditing all the Quranic resources you need for your project, we've curated and open-sourced them. We've also included the tools necessary to interact with and explore them.


QUL is primarily geared towards other Muslim developers. To contribute, visit the QUL repo on [Github](https://github.com/TarteelAI/quranic-universal-library).
Special thanks to [all those whose contributions made this project possible](https://qul.tarteel.ai/credits).
