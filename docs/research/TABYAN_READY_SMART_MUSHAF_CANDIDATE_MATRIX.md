# Ready Smart Mushaf Candidate Matrix — Tabyan

**Research date:** 2026-10-15  
**Shortlist size:** 3 technical renderer candidates; none is rights-cleared  
**Decision rule:** Code, Quran text, page layout, fonts, audio, and models are evaluated separately. A reader or recitation provider alone does not satisfy the complete-stack requirement.

## Other screened leads (not shortlisted)

- **Quran.ws Engine:** technically relevant word masking and 604-page data, but the docs say the engine binaries and installable package are not published. Its license page mirrors a KFGQPC statement permitting digital publishing and software/web use, but this scan did not verify that statement directly with the rights holder or establish precise commercial app-store/white-label scope. [[18]](https://quran.ws/docs/reference/quran-engine/) [[19]](https://quran.ws/docs/reference/licensing/)
- **Itqan IMAD:** the app requires more than 9,000 page images distributed separately; the repository MIT license does not license those images, and word-level hide/reveal is not demonstrated. [[20]](https://github.com/Itqan-community/mushaf-imad-flutter) [[21]](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Itqan-community/mushaf-imad-flutter/main/LICENSE)
- **Quran Muaalem:** batch inference project, not a turnkey hosted live provider; visible model and dataset evidence does not establish training-data or source-audio rights. [[22]](https://obadx.github.io/quran-muaalem/en/muaalem/python-api.html) [[23]](https://huggingface.co/obadx/muaalem-model-v3_2) [[24]](https://huggingface.co/datasets/obadx/muaalem-annotated-v3)
- **Smartech:** public product page describes a Quran-learning app, but no embeddable SDK, white-label grant, or distribution terms were located. [[25]](https://smartech.online/solutions/smart-mushaf)
- **Quran Foundation content alone / Tanzil text alone:** useful content rights paths, not standalone Smart Mushaf renderers or recitation providers. [[10]](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/) [[26]](https://tanzil.net/docs/text_license)

## Decision

No candidate in this matrix is approved as a rights-clear ready Smart Mushaf or ready Mushaf-plus-provider stack. The three ranked rows are technical investigation leads only; the QF row is a comparison architecture, and `quran_library` is excluded for monetized Tasmee under the included model license.
