# Which memorization features does Tarteel Premium unlock?

Tarteel Premium unlocks these memorization features:

- **Mistake Detection**: our flagship feature. Hide the ayahs, recite from memory, and Tarteel alerts you in real time when you miss, add or change a word. See [How to activate Mistake Detection](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414419).

- **Tashkeel mistakes**: Tarteel can also flag mistakes in the vowel marks (tashkeel). See [How to turn on Tashkeel recognition](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414420).

- **Verse Peeking**: when you are stuck while reciting with hidden ayahs, reveal the next word or the next ayah. Tarteel records the words you peeked, so you know which words to review.

- **Mistake history, frequency and playback**: see every past mistake, how often you make it, and listen to your own recording of each mistake.

- **Color similar phrases (mutashabihat)** on the Quran page, so you can see which phrases repeat in other verses.


# What does Tarteel Premium add to Testing Mode?

Testing Mode is free with a limited number of test rounds each day, and every user gets the "Suggested for you" tests. Tarteel Premium adds:

- **Unlimited test rounds** every day.

- **Test duration setting**, to choose how long a test lasts.

- **Question length setting**, to make each question up to 15 lines long.


See [Getting started with Testing](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/16557456).

# Which progress and listening features does Tarteel Premium unlock?

Tarteel Premium also unlocks these progress and listening features:

- **Unlimited session history, session audio and audio sharing**. On the free plan, you can see and play only your last session.

- **Advanced analytics and a mistakes overview** of your memorization progress.

- **Unlimited goals**. The free plan includes 1 goal.

- **Add sessions you did outside Tarteel**, so your progress includes them.

- **Session pausing** during a recitation session.

- **Word-by-word highlighting** when you listen to a reciter. On the free plan, Tarteel highlights ayah by ayah.

- **Recent search history of 25 searches**. The free plan keeps 3.


# What does Tarteel Premium not include?

Tarteel does not detect Tajweed mistakes yet, on the free plan or on Premium. See [Tarteel is not recognising my Tajweed mistakes](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414457).
