# Terms and acceptable use

Usage expectations for the Nabr recitation API, data restrictions, abuse prevention, service limits, and responsible implementation.

Current working terms

Last updated May 5, 2026. This page is a product-facing terms summary and should be reviewed by counsel before contractual use.

## Permitted use

- Use Nabr to build Quran learning, recitation assessment, voice search, tajweed feedback, makharij analysis, research, and school support workflows.
- Keep the teacher and institution responsible for final learner guidance, religious instruction, and certification decisions.
- Follow API documentation, rate limits, authentication rules, and deployment-specific operating instructions.

## Data restrictions

- Do not send data unless you have the rights, notices, and permissions required for the learners, teachers, schools, or users involved.
- Do not use Nabr output as an automatic religious ruling, certification, or replacement for qualified teacher review.
- Do not resell, sublicense, benchmark publicly, or use returned data to train competing systems unless an agreement explicitly allows it.

## Abuse prevention

- Do not attempt to bypass limits, reverse engineer protected systems, overload the API, or interfere with other customers.
- Do not submit malicious files, unlawful content, or audio collected without appropriate consent.
- Nabr may suspend access for security risks, abusive traffic, payment issues, or violations of acceptable use.

## Service limits

- Model output can be affected by recording quality, background noise, recitation style, unsupported content, and integration settings.
- Customers are responsible for testing the experience in their own product, language, school, and learner context before production rollout.
- Commercial terms, service levels, support commitments, data retention, and local deployment details should be defined in a separate agreement.

For API terms, usage limits, or school deployment requirements, contact the Nabr team.
