# How do I customize the settings?

September 25, 2025

Whether you're choosing to Recite or Listen, customizing your settings in each mode makes it that much easier to engage with Tarteel in the way you want.

1. Tap the Settings icon in the top right hand of your screen.

2. You will find both Recitation and Listening under the Experiences section.

3. Choose your preferences for either setting.
