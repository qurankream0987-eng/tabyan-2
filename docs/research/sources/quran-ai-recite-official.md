## Study the Quran at any level.

Whether you are new to the Quran or have studied it for years, use quran.ai to work through a passage or discover connections you had not noticed. Keep the mushaf in view as you ask, compare, and follow the evidence back to the text.

Why does this word take that ending? How does Ibn Ashur explain the passage’s structure? Where else does the Quran tell this part of Musa’s story, and what changes in the telling? Start with your question and study it in depth.

### Established tafsir comes first.

The point is to study what the sources say, not to have an AI agree with you. quran.ai retrieves verified Quran text, named tafsir, translations, and Arabic linguistic evidence. Its grounding rules require citations, preserve scholarly differences, and distinguish retrieved commentary from the AI’s own synthesis. The text stays in view, and the answer can be checked against its sources.

Draw on **20 tafsir editions**, from al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir to al-Zamakhshari and Ibn Ashur, alongside **31 translations across 12 languages**, word-by-word meanings, and detailed Arabic grammatical analysis. Sourced from **quran.com and Quran Foundation**.
