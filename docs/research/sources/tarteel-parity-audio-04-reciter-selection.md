# How to switch between reciters in listening mode?

Updated this week

Use this article to see which reciters Tarteel has and to change the reciter in listening mode. Tarteel has **16 recitations from 13 reciters**. All Tarteel reciters are free for every user, and you can download any of them to listen offline.

Some reciters have more than one style of recitation:

- **Murattal**: a steady, moderate pace, good for daily reading and memorization.

- **Mujawwad**: a slower, melodic style with longer pauses.

- **Muallim**: a teaching style, where the reciter recites slowly and clearly so you can repeat after him.


The reciters in Tarteel are:

- Abdul Basit Abd us-Samad (Murattal and Mujawwad)

- Abdul-Rahman Al-Sudais

- Abu Bakr Al-Shatri

- Ahmad Alnufais

- Khalifa Al-Tunaiji

- Maher Al-Muaiqly

- Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary (Murattal, Mujawwad and Muallim)

- Mansour Al Salimi

- Mishari Al-Afasy

- Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawy (Murattal)

- Saad Al-Ghamdi

- Saud Al-Shuraim

- Yasser Al-Dosari


If you wish to change the reciter in listening mode, please follow the steps below:

- From the Quran Reader, long tap any Ayah

- Tap on "Listen"

- Under the Playback settings, and at the reciter, tap on the arrow down button to the right side of the reciter's name

- Choose the reciter you want and tap on "Start"
