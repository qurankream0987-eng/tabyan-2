# How to listen to your recitation recordings and mistakes

April 2, 2026

Session recordings and mistake audio playback are **Premium-only** features. If you are on the free tier, you will not be able to play back recitation recordings or mistake audio.

# Listen to a Full Session Recording

1. Open Tarteel on your device.

2. Go to the **Activity** tab.

3. Tap **Session History**.

4. Select a session that was recorded in **Recitation mode**.

5. Tap **Play** to listen to the full recording.


# Listen to Specific Mistake Audio

1. Open Tarteel on your device.

2. Go to the **Activity** tab.

3. Tap **Session History**.

4. Open the **Mistakes** tab.

5. Select the session you’d like to review and tap the mistake count

6. Tap **Play** to hear the mistake audio.

7. Tap the reciter button next to it to hear the correct recitation.


# Important Notes

- **Premium required:** Session playback and mistake audio playback are available exclusively to Premium subscribers.

- **Recording must be enabled:** Before starting a recitation session, make sure recording is enabled. Go to **Settings** \> **Data Usage**, then toggle on **Save audio data**.

- **Recitation mode only:** Playback is available only for sessions recorded in **Recitation mode**.


# Troubleshooting

If you cannot play back your recordings or mistakes, one possible cause is that the audio is from a period before you subscribed, or between subscription renewals. Recordings for non-Premium users are regularly wiped.

If that does not apply and you still cannot play back your recordings, try the following:

1. Update to the latest version of the Tarteel app.

2. Restart the app, then try again.

3. If the issue persists, contact our support team and include a short screen recording showing the problem so we can investigate.
