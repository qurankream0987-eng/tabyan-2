What is the Surah Selector?

The Surah Selector is a feature in Tarteel that allows you to quickly jump to any Surah (chapter), Juz (section), or Hizb (part) within the Quran. This feature is designed to enhance your memorization and recitation experience by providing an organized and efficient way to navigate the Quran through Tarteel.

Benefits of Using the Surah Selector:

- Efficiency: Quickly find and navigate to any part of the Quran without scrolling through pages.

- Flexibility: Choose between viewing Surahs, Juz, or Hizb and organize them in the order that suits you best.

- Convenience: Especially useful for targeted memorization or when following along with a teacher or study group.


Tips for Optimal Use:

- Frequent Navigation: If you often jump between Surahs, Juz, and Hizb, familiarize yourself with their names and numbers to save time.

- Combined Use: Use the Surah Selector in conjunction with other Tarteel features like voice search and real-time feedback for a comprehensive Quranic study experience.


Tarteel's Surah Selector is designed to enhance your Quranic journey by providing easy and efficient navigation through the holy text. Whether you are memorizing, reciting, or studying, this feature ensures that you can quickly and effortlessly access any Surah, Juz, or Hizb. Happy reading!
