# How to download reciters’ audio for offline listening?

September 25, 2025

You can download your favorite reciter's audio amongst the selection that Tarteel has, and listen offline for your convenience. In order to do that, please follow the steps below:

- Long tap any ayah in the Quran reader

- From the pop-up menu, choose "Listen"

- From the Listen menu, tap on "Manage Downloads" under your chosen Reciter

- This will open all the available reciters

- If you want to download the whole mushaf, tap on "Download All"

- If you want a certain Surah(s), then tap on the Arrow down that is available on the left side of the Reciter's name and then tap on the Green arrow down that is available on the right side of the Surah you want to download!
