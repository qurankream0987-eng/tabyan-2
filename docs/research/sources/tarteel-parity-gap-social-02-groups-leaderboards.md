# Groups and Leaderboards

September 25, 2025

Ready to compete for some good deeds? Our Groups and Leaderboards feature allows you to get your loved ones together and engage with the Quran as a community! This way you can motivate one another, have some friendly competition and aim for the top spot on our global leaderboard which resets every Islamic month.

Our leaderboards are organized in two ways; engagement and completions. Engagement refers to the time any group's participants spend reading or reciting the Quran using Tarteel (this includes offline sessions for Premium users) which will be added to the hours shown in the leaderboard table. The group with the most hours for the month takes the #1 spot. Completions refer to the amount of khatmahs read or recited by each group.
