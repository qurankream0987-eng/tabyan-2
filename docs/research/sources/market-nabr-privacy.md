Current working policy

Last updated May 5, 2026. This page summarizes the policy as it applies to the product. It should be reviewed by legal counsel before it is used in a contract.

## 01Data we may process

1. 1.1
Recitation audio and the feedback derived from it, as needed to run voice search, recitation assessment, tajweed feedback, makharij analysis and related Quran learning workflows.

2. 1.2
API request metadata, such as timestamps, session identifiers, product configuration, error codes and operational logs, needed to keep the service reliable.

3. 1.3
Business contact details shared with us through demo requests, support conversations, school onboarding or research collaboration discussions.


## 02How data is used

1. 2.1
To provide the requested product features, including audio processing, session setup, structured feedback and technical support.

2. 2.2
To monitor reliability, prevent abuse, debug integration issues, and improve the quality of documentation and deployments.

3. 2.3
To improve models or evaluation workflows, but only when the customer agreement, consent flow or research arrangement permits that use.


## 03School and learner data

1. 3.1
Schools and product teams are responsible for telling their learners, parents, teachers and administrators clearly how recitation data is collected and used.

2. 3.2
Nabr should be implemented with the minimum data the learning workflow needs, especially when children or classrooms are involved.

3. 3.3
Depending on an institution's needs, a customer deployment can require local operation, access control, retention limits or additional privacy terms.


## 04Security and retention

1. 4.1
Depending on the deployment model, access should be limited to authorized operations, support and research staff.

2. 4.2
Retention should be set according to the customer's workflow, legal requirements and any permission to use data for model improvement.

3. 4.3
Customers can contact Nabr to discuss data deletion, export, access limits and security controls specific to their deployment.


For privacy questions, school deployment requirements or data-processing terms, contact the Nabr team.
