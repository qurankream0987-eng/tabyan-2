**Full Description:**

Join **15+ million Muslims** strengthening their relationship with the Quran.

Tarteel is the leading AI-powered Quran companion designed to help you memorize, read, and review with confidence. Whether you are a student of Hifz or someone looking to improve their daily recitation, Tarteel adapts to your journey.

**THE #1 AI QURAN COMPANION**

Simply hide the verses and recite; Tarteel uses industry-leading recognition accuracy to track your voice and reveal the words on the screen _as you recite them_. It’s not just about reading—it’s about verifying your memorization instantly.

**REINFORCE YOUR MEMORIZATION**

• **Memorization Mistake Detection:** Hide the verses on the mushaf and recite from memory. Tarteel’s AI listens to your recitation and flags word-level errors, skipped words, or incorrect tashkeel instantly.

• **Recite Anywhere, Anytime:** Whether you are in the car, walking to work, or whispering in a quiet room, Tarteel follows your recitation. No need to wait for a revision partner. Your Quran companion is always ready.

• **Live Correction:** Tap on any highlighted mistake to see exactly what you recited versus the correct Ayah. Build confidence before your next class.

**PLAN, TRACK & IMPROVE**

• **Smart Goals:** Set custom targets for memorization, revision, or reading (e.g., "Surah Al-Kahf every Friday" or "Review Juz 30").

• **Advanced Analytics:** Visualize your progress with streaks and heatmaps. See which Surahs are strong and which Ayahs need more work.

• **Unlimited Listening:** Listen to your favorite Qaris to perfect your pronunciation and rhythm. Follow along with the text or listen in the background—completely free.

**KEY FEATURES**

• **Voice Search:** "Shazam for Quran"—recite any phrase, and Tarteel will instantly find the specific Ayah and Surah.

• **Adaptive Mushaf:** Choose between Madani or Indo-Pak scripts, with options to hide Ayah markers for a true test of memory.

• **Historical Mistakes:** Review a log of your past recitation errors to identify patterns and weak spots in your Hifz.

• **Privacy First:** Tarteel does not sell your data. We operate with Ihsan, prioritizing user privacy and experience.

**TARTEEL PREMIUM**

While recitation follow-along, listening, and voice search are free, Tarteel Premium unlocks the full power of AI. Start your **FREE TRIAL** to access:

• Live Memorization Mistake Detection

• Peeking & Hidden Verses Mode

• Goal Planning & Advanced Analytics

• Historical Mistake Tracking

Join the global community of muslims using Tarteel. Memorize smarter, stay consistent, and correct your recitation instantly.
