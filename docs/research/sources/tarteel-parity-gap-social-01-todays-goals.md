# What are “Today's Goals”?

September 25, 2025

Just below your Highlight cards, you'll notice a section of your Dashboard titled Today's Goals. This section lists all of the Goals you've set in the Goals tab and serves as a reminder to engage with your daily portion and will take you straight to it, whether it be reading, recitation, revision or even new memorization.
