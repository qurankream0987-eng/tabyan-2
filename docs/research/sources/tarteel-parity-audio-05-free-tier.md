# Can I use Tarteel for free?

Use this article to see what you can do in Tarteel for free and what Tarteel Premium adds. **Tarteel is free to use**, with no ads, on as many devices as you want. Tarteel Premium is a monthly or annual subscription that adds AI memorization tools, such as Mistake Detection and unlimited Testing Mode.

# What can I do in Tarteel for free?

Every Tarteel user can use these features for free:

- **Unlimited recitation follow-along**: recite out loud, and Tarteel follows your place in the Quran.

- **AI Voice Search**: recite any part of an ayah, or let Tarteel hear someone else recite, and Tarteel finds the verse.

- **Testing Mode**, with a limited number of test rounds each day, including the "Suggested for you" tests.

- **Hide ayahs**, to recite from memory with the verses hidden.

- **152 Quran translations in 76 languages**, plus English transliteration.

- **45 tafsirs in 20 languages**.

- **16 recitations from 13 reciters**, with offline downloads, repeat and custom ranges.

- **Tajweed colors** in the Mushaf.

- **4 Mushaf layouts**: Madani (1405, 1421 and 1441) and IndoPak.

- **Similar phrases and similar verses (mutashabihat) lists** for any ayah.

- **Streaks, basic analytics, badges, bookmarks and 1 goal**.

- **Sync across all your devices**.


# What does Tarteel Premium add?

Tarteel Premium adds AI memorization tools on top of everything in the free plan. The biggest ones are **Mistake Detection**, where Tarteel alerts you in real time when you make a mistake while you recite from memory, and **unlimited Testing Mode**. For the full list, see [What features do I unlock with Tarteel Premium?](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414460)

# What does Tarteel not do yet?

Tarteel does not detect Tajweed mistakes yet, on the free plan or on Premium. See [Tarteel is not recognising my Tajweed mistakes](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414457).

# How do I get Tarteel Premium?

You can subscribe to Tarteel Premium monthly or annually in the app or at [tarteel.ai/pricing](https://tarteel.ai/pricing). Your subscription also helps the Tarteel team keep developing and improving the app.

If you cannot afford Tarteel Premium, see [I can't afford the Premium Subscription, what can I do?](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414383)
