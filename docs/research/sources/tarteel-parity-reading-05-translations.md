Tarteel has **152 Quran translations in 76 languages**, plus an English transliteration. All Tarteel translations are **free for every user**, and you can download them to read offline. The full list of languages is at the end of this article.

English alone has 21 translations in Tarteel, including Dr. Mustafa Khattab (The Clear Quran), Sahih International, Abdullah Yusuf Ali, Pickthall, Muhammad Asad, Abdel Haleem and Mufti Taqi Usmani. Urdu has 8 translations, and Bengali, Russian and Turkish have 5 each.

This article explains how to enable and use translations in the Tarteel app, allowing you to accompany the Arabic text with translations for better understanding and study.

# **How to View and Add Translations**

1. **Access Settings:**





   - Open the Tarteel app.

   - Go to the "Settings" menu.


2. **Select Translations:**





   - Under the "Downloads" section, you will find a range of available translations.

   - Select the translations you want to download and use.


**Using Translations in Quran Reader Mode (View All Translations):**

1. In the Quran Reader mode, press and hold down on a verse.

2. A menu will appear; select the "Translations" option.

3. The selected translation(s) will be displayed for the verse.


**Using Translations in Adaptive Mode (Single Translation View):**

- In Adaptive Mode, only one translation can be visible at a time unless you press and hold, and select Translation from the menu that appears, just like in the Quran Reader view.

- The selected translation will appear below the Arabic text, allowing for a seamless reading experience


Want the explanation of an ayah, not only its meaning? Tarteel also has 45 tafsirs in 20 languages. Press and hold an ayah and tap **Tafsir**.
