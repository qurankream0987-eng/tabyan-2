**There are 2 main ways to change the Quran reader settings:**

1- Through the Main menu

- From the Dashboard, tap on the settings gear icon on the top right

- Scroll down to the "Page Layout" Section

- Tap on "Adaptive mode"

- From there, you can increase or decrease the font and adjust the other settings to your convenience


2- From the Quran Reader:

- Tap anywhere on the screen to show the Top menu bar

- From there, Tap on the "Settings" gear icon on the top right

- Scroll down to the "Page Layout" section

- Similar to method #1, you can adjust the settings to your convenience!

# Which Mushaf layouts does Tarteel have?

Tarteel has **4 Mushaf layouts**. All Tarteel Mushaf layouts have 15 lines per page and are free for every user:

- **Madani Mushaf (1405)**: the original layout, widely used around the world (604 pages)

- **Madani Mushaf (1421)**: an updated edition with refined line breaks and ayah positions (604 pages)

- **Madani Mushaf (1441)**: the latest edition. Surahs do not start at the end of a page, and lines do not start with ayah markers (604 pages)

- **IndoPak Mushaf (Naskh)**: the IndoPak script (610 pages)


To change the Mushaf layout in Tarteel:

1. Tap **Settings**.

2. Under **Quran Appearance**, tap **Mushaf Layout**.

3. Choose the Mushaf that matches the one you recite and memorize from. The Quran reader then shows the Mushaf you chose.


You can also turn on [Tajweed colors](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/14936922-understanding-tajweed-colors-in-the-tarteel-mushaf) in the Mushaf, free for all users.
