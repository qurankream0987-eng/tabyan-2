# Privacy policy

How Nabr handles recitation audio, API data, school workflows, contact information, and customer responsibilities.

Current working policy

Last updated May 5, 2026. This page is a product-facing policy summary and should be reviewed by counsel before contractual use.

## Data we may process

- Recitation audio and derived feedback needed to run voice search, recitation assessment, tajweed feedback, makharij analysis, and related Quran learning workflows.
- API request metadata such as timestamps, session identifiers, product configuration, error codes, and operational logs needed to keep the service reliable.
- Business contact details shared through demo requests, support conversations, school onboarding, or research collaboration discussions.

## How data is used

- To provide requested product functionality, including audio processing, session setup, structured feedback, and technical support.
- To monitor reliability, prevent abuse, debug integration issues, and improve documentation and deployment quality.
- To improve models or evaluation workflows only when the customer agreement, consent flow, or research arrangement permits that use.

## School and learner data

- Schools and product teams are responsible for giving their learners, parents, teachers, and administrators clear notices about how recitation data is collected and used.
- Nabr should be implemented with the minimum data needed for the learning workflow, especially when children or classroom environments are involved.
- Customer deployments can require local operation, access control, retention limits, or additional privacy terms depending on institutional needs.

## Security and retention

- Access should be limited to authorized operational, support, and research personnel based on the deployment model.
- Retention should be configured around the customer workflow, legal requirements, and model-improvement permissions.
- Customers can contact Nabr to discuss deletion, export, access limits, and deployment-specific security controls.

For privacy questions, school deployment requirements, or data-processing terms, contact the Nabr team.
