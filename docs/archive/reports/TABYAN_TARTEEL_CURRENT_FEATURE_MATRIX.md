# Tabyan–Tarteel Current Quran Feature Matrix

**Audit date:** 2026-10-07  
**Scope:** The 68 feature groups in the supplied parity specification, compared with Tarteel's publicly documented product behavior and Tabyan's current source/docs. Documentation-only audit; no product, provider, data, policy, or deployment changes are authorized here.

## How to read this audit

- Status describes Tabyan's current functional coverage of the specified feature group, not an assertion that Tabyan's and Tarteel's internals are alike.
- **PARITY** means the requested core behavior exists in the audited surface. It does not prove device-level microphone accuracy, availability on every platform, or Tarteel-equivalent quality.
- **PARTIAL** means a meaningful subset exists but one or more material requirements, roles, platforms, or persistence paths are absent or unproven.
- **MISSING** means the requested capability is not present in the audited Tabyan Quran experience.
- **BETTER_IN_TABYAN** means a deliberate Tabyan product advantage or fit, not a measured claim that Tabyan is objectively better than Tarteel.
- **NOT_APPLICABLE** is reserved for a feature that does not apply to Tabyan's product contract. No group in this supplied list met that test.
- **BLOCKED** means a named evidence, safety, privacy, licensing, or authoritative-data gate must be resolved before implementation is responsible.
- Sources are numbered in the final source index and in `research/tarteel-feature-parity/sources.json`. A Tarteel first-party source proves what Tarteel publicly says, not independent recognition accuracy or actual availability for every account, plan, locale, or rollout.
- Tabyan statements are code/document observations. They do not substitute for signed-in end-to-end, provider, or physical-device acceptance.

## Evidence and confidence limits

1. The public-source set contains 34 distinct URLs after deduplicating the two Google Play locale variants. Every source is first-party Tier 3 evidence; there are no independent accuracy or product-availability studies in this audit.
2. Current-week Help Center copy and the 2026-09-17 Google Play listing were checked on 2026-10-07. The Apple listing was retrieved on the same date. Search snippets are explicitly labeled and used only for the statements they show.
3. A material plan conflict remains: the Help Center says Hide ayahs is free, while Apple/Google listing copy includes peeking/hidden verses in Premium-related copy. No single plan rule is asserted.
4. The privacy policy is dated 2023-05-26. It is too old to establish current retention, data-region, recording-collection or user-control behavior.
5. The official API answer is dated 2025-09-27. A current-week news sweep found no clear later Tarteel API announcement, but that does not prove no newer or private API exists.
6. Thin public evidence remains for test scoring and interruption controls, goal-credit rules, analytics/heatmaps, bookmark persistence, teacher workflows, exact multilingual positioning, and subscription behavior by account/platform.
7. Tabyan evidence is strongest for the Web QCF reader, local test/history/search/audio behavior and source-level separation of modes. Native Mushaf, physical microphone accuracy, offline behavior, real-account sync, and provider acceptance were not proven by this audit.
8. Tarteel promotional claims are not treated as accuracy benchmarks. ASR confidence is not an educational score; a raw mismatch is not a confirmed Quran mistake; Tajweed colors do not prove tashkeel detection.
