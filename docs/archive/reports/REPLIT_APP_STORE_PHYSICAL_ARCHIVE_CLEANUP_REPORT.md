# Replit Support Diagnosis

Replit Support identified the App Store publishing failure as:

```text
413 PAYLOAD TOO LARGE
```

The App Store archive is affected by physical workspace content, even when that content is listed in `.replitignore`. This audit therefore measured and reduced physical files rather than relying on `.replitignore`.

## Why .replitignore Was Insufficient

The supplied support diagnosis states that `.replitignore` does not reduce the archive used by Replit App Store / Expo Launch publishing. It remains useful for other Replit deployment paths, but it is not treated as a size reduction for the App Store archive in this report.
